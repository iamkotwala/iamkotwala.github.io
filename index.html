I work at the intersection of finance, data, and business systems. I build the data architecture and automated reporting that turn fragmented operational information into reliable financial insight — integrating accounting ERP, CRM and operational databases, and replacing manual reporting with automated pipelines in Power Query, SQL and Power BI.

At Regent College London (Regent India Pvt Ltd), I redesigned financial reporting end-to-end — cutting a recurring 3-day manual process to a ~5-minute automated run, raising accuracy from ~70% to ~99%, and moving reporting from monthly to weekly. Earlier, at AIS Info, I built financial models for U.S. bankruptcy debt-restructuring; before that I worked in cloud infrastructure at TCS for a U.S. enterprise client.

I hold a Master's in Data Science from Jai Hind College, University of Mumbai, and a Bachelor's in Computer Applications (Hons.) from Maharaja Sayajirao University, Vadodara.
